01

Fix before you spend more

Found while reviewing the profile, landing page and Ads Manager on 19 Oct.

Blocker

No Meta Pixel on interviewtrix.com

The landing page loads GA4 and Clarity only; fbq is undefined and interview-fe has no pixel code. Without it Meta can't optimise for registrations, can't build retargeting audiences of page visitors, and you can't see cost per registration. Add the pixel tonight and fire CompleteRegistration on sign-up.

Blocker

Ads Manager account isn't set up

Ad account 1769948170688665 shows "Get set up to run ads" and has no campaigns. The ad you started is an in-app Instagram Boost, which can only optimise for profile visits, messages or link clicks. Finish Account overview (business info + payment method, which you add yourself) so you can run the campaigns below.

Fix

Dates don't match across touchpoints

Instagram posts say 20–26 Oct; the landing page says 20–27 Oct and an "8-day hackathon". Pick one deadline (27 Oct, 11:59 PM IST) and use it in every ad, story and caption so last-call ads are believable.

Fix

The "₹799 → FREE, offer ends in 8h, limited seats" block

The countdown runs out tonight while registration stays open for a week, and the strikethrough price was never charged. Meta can reject ads that send traffic to misleading urgency claims. Replace with "Free · Registration closes 27 Oct".

Fix

Prize numbers

Ads say "₹20,000 prize pool"; the three prize cards add up to about ₹25,800 in listed value, and the pool counter renders ₹0 INR until it animates on scroll. Render the final number server-side and keep "₹20,000+" consistent.

Good

Profile is ready for paid traffic

Bio link points at the hackathon, three on-brand hackathon posts are pinned, and "WIN ₹20K" / "Winning Products" highlights exist. Bio link uses /hackathon-2026, which redirects; send ads straight to /hiring-games-hackathon-2026.

05

Creative set

Make each concept in 9:16 (Reels, Stories) and 4:5 (Feed). First 2 seconds carry the hook as on-screen text, since most people watch muted. Keep text off the top and bottom 14% of 9:16 frames.

REEL 15–20s · founder to camera

"Can you beat our AI interviewer?"

Hook: "I built an AI that interviews you. Bet you can't score 80."

Cut to the AI interview screen, a score popping up, then prize cards and "Free · ends 27 Oct". Your existing post with the ChatGPT × InterviewTrix face-cam is the right style.

REEL 12s · screen recording

Live score demo

Hook: "This is what an AI interviewer thinks of your answer."

Real UI: question appears, candidate answers, instant feedback and score. Product-led ads tend to bring higher-intent sign-ups.

CAROUSEL 4:5 · 5 cards

What you can win

One card per prize tier, then "How to win in 3 steps", then CTA card. Works well in Facebook Feed.

MetricWhereHealthyAct if

Registration → completed both interviewsYour DB / GA4≥ 30%Low → reminder email/WhatsApp + "how to" story
Frequency (retargeting)Ad set< 3 / week> 3 → swap creative

ScaleWhen it works

CPR at or below target for 2 days → raise budget 20–30% once a day. Don't double it in one go; it resets learning.

KillWhen it doesn't

Pause individual ads, not the whole ad set. Keep at least 3 live ads per ad set.

HoldFirst 48 hours

No edits to targeting, budget or creative while the ad set is in learning, unless an ad is rejected.
